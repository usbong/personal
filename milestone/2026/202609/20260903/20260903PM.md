Okamoto-san tells us that Evergrande Group is a very large group from China that sells real estate. Furthermore, it's said that this group has caused the collapse of the real estate industry in China by committing several crimes such as not paying taxes, transferring around 1.2 trillion yen to the founder's ex-wife, Ding Yumei, while saying that these were dividends from another company in the Caiman Islands.

Okamoto-san also explains that, in the first place, the group has done something very similar to what has happened in Johor, Malaysia, in which they develop housing units where many people could live. However, in this case, the same with the situation in Malaysia, there remained many housing units where nobody lives. Moreover, many years pass and these housing units are deteriorating because of things like the rain.

Okamoto-san next points out that, in general, the developer must first get half of the payment in order to create the housing unit, and, in addition, there's the expectation that the value of these housing units is going to go up much later. Unfortunately, this isn't what happened in this case.

Finally, the founder, Hui Ka Yan, has been imprisoned for life, while his ex-wife, Ding Yumei, still isn't, though their eldest son has been imprisoned for 18 years and their second son, for one year and 10 months.
