> Stories from Game Producer Yoshiki Okamoto: Former ping-pong athlete Mr. Jun Mizutani has lost at most 600 million yen by investing in FX as well as the stock market

> Writer: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-09-10

## --

Dear friends,

Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto's talk about former ping-pong athlete Mr. Jun Mizutani, who's lost at most 600 million yen by investing in FX as well as the stock market.

According to Okamoto-san, Mizutani-san shares this pastime of his on social media, although it seems that he isn't very skillful in winning. Okamoto-san thus thinks that Mizutani-san invests small amounts every month like payments in order to experience the thrill of winning or losing, something that, Okamoto-san says, is related to dopamine as well as adrenaline. Nonetheless, for the most part, he loses, so much so that he's lost millions of yen.

Okamoto-san next points out, for instance, the shares of Micron Technology in which an investor would have been able to obtain 12 million yen from an investment of 8.2 million yen. Furthermore, Okamoto-san teaches that it's an experience similar to playing Rock, Paper, Scissors wherein the players bet a million to win two or three times more.

However, Okamoto-san thinks that Mizutani-san isn't very capable of winning through investing, because he's lost so much in only eight years, and Okamoto-san believes that Mizutani-san would have been able to win more easily in the S&P stockmarket. While Okamoto-san admits that he himself loses in the stock market, it's only when he sells the shares that he really loses money. Therefore, he does it in a period of 10 years. In particular, he emphasizes that he buys when the value has fallen, and sells when the value is high.

Finally, Okamoto-san wants us to focus on the mentality of a person like Mizutani-san, who stays strong despite losing so much money.

This is all.
