> Stories from Game Producer Yoshiki Okamoto: The things that we've learned from playing video games

> Writer: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-09-16

## --

Dear friends,

Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto, Ms. Tanu and Ms. Tapi's talk about the things that they've learned from playing video games.

## --

First, Ms. Tanu shares with us that she's currently playing Monster Hunter NOW, and that she's been able to learn the names of several weapons, something that， Okamoto-san， admits might not seem useful in the real world. Tanu-san then gives another example, which is Catan, wherein the ability to foresee the future instead of focusing solely on the present is highly valued.

Ms. Tapi, for her part, reveals that she's been able to learn the names of the stations of the train as well as the special products of the places all over Japan by playing Momotaro Dentetsu. 

She also mentions that from the Street Fighter II character Dhalsim, she's learned the idea of stretching the body more than what is known to be the limit. 

With regard to this point, Okamoto-san recounts that in Hawaii, he's met people who blow fire with their mouths, and he thinks that it's really something that isn't as difficult to do as we might think. However, he admits that, in reality, there are no green-skinned people who can generate electricity with their bodies in Brazil.

Next, Ms. Tapi also gives the game, Sangoku Muso, in which we can get to know well the names of very famous military leaders written about in literature. However, Okamoto-san mentions that all of these leaders are depicted as handsome, something that isn't necessarily true.

Finally, since Okamoto-san asks us what we've learned through playing video games, I would thus like to say that the first time that I played Street Fighter II was when I was still in grade school around the year 1994, and it was the version for the Super Famicom console. Therefore, I think that it was in those years that I was able to get to know more about the creative products from Japan.
