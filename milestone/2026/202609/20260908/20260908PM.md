Okamoto-san recounts that according to the news on social media, Kurumada-san has been embezzled by his management team a very large amount costing some 4.6 billion yen.

Okamoto-san tells us that his team of managers has invested in cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin for Master Kurumada. However, in recent years, the value of these cryptocurrencies has fallen significantly. As a result, people such as Kurumada-san have lost a lot of money as well.

Despite this outcome, Okamoto-san mentions that the managers have been able to return 1.8 billion yen to Kurumada-san, something that lets him enjoy life, although not as much if they had been able to return the entire sum.

Finally, Okamoto-san recalls Kurumada-san's manga comic books wherein there can be found fights between beings with superpowers capable of destroying buildings. Furthermore, they fight each other five against five. Since then, manga comic books about fights have become very popular.
