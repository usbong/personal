> Hitachi, Nokia-made drones to assess infrastructure, while automatically flying in the air

> Author: Takushi Yoshida

> Translator: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-09-08

## --

Hitachi Manufacturing has announced on September 7th that it has acquired for the first time the rights to sell in Japan the drones developed by Finland's big telco equipment maker, Nokia, in order to strengthen its services related to assessing situations during times of calamity and inspecting large-scale infrastructures.

Nokia's drones have been equipped to automatically fly in the air, land on its own as well as attach itself to a charging port at the base station. 

Hitachi is automating its inspection capabilities, which are linked to man-power-related savings and safety, by combining Nokia's drones with its existing drone transport management system to inspect power facilities.

When disaster occurs, drones could be made to fly toward far-away places like mountaneous regions and remote islands that are difficult for humans to reach, and are therefore capable of quickly assessing the damage.

Aside from businesses that have large-scale facilities related to power, steel and chemicals, their main customers are expected to also include Local Government Units such as the police and the fire departments, which respond during times of disaster, with the goal of making 10 contracts in one year.

Nokia's drones where introduced in 70 areas in Belgium, and is also widely used in America. They are actively used by the police and the fire departments in public safety.
