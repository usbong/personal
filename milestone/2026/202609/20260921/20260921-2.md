> "Boy King" who led the ancient kingdom of Uganda at 3 years old  has passed away. His successor, a newscaster?

> Translator: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-09-21

## --

Registered in the Guinness Book of World Records as the "World's Youngest King," King Oyo Nyimba has passed away at 34 years old on August 27th. Born in April 1992, he immediately succeeded the throne in September 1995. Local news media "Monitor" says that King Oyo died while he was staying in America to undergo treatment for cancer. In September 9 of this year, it was announced during a meeting hosted by the royal family that his cousin, Prince Edward Lukidi Kijanango (56), a newscaster from the national broadcasting agency, has been chosen to be his successor. However, close relatives argue that "King Oyo has a son."
