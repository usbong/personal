> 259th Priest of Tendai-sect Koken Fuji passes away at 94

> Translator: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-09-16

## --

Born in Nagasaki Prefecture, Koken Fuji graduated from Taisho University, and served as the Head Priest at Konjo-in in Yoshinogari Town, Saga Prefecture afterward. He's previously served as the Conference Chair of Tendai-sect, the Secretary-General of Religious Affairs and the Head Priest of the Manshu-in Monzeki Temple in Kyoto, among other positions. In February 2025, he was appointed to be the Head Priest of the Tendai-sect. With the passing of Mr. Fuji, Somon Horisawa (97) from the Enryaki-ji Temple in Taimon-an has been appointed to be the 260th Head Priest of the Tendai-sect.
