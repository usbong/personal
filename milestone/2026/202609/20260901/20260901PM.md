> Publication Date: 2026-09-02

Dear friends,

Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto's lecture with Ms. Tanu about the new "Dragon Quest" board game.

First, Tanu-chan informs Okamoto-san that it's a Jinsei game (or a Game of Life-type of game), the same with games like Monopoly or Catan, wherein, Okamoto-san admits, a person like himself could experience things that he can never experience in real life.

Okamoto-san next points out that the price of the game is foreseen to go up two or three times more in the shops owned by resellers, because they take advantage of this new Dragon Quest release by buying many with the goal of reselling them later; thus, there are general consumers who won't be able to buy one when the new game is finally released on October 24th of this year at 7,150 yen.

Okamoto-san also reveals that, for his part, he wants to buy two or three, in which he'll play one, while hold on to the rest. Okamoto-san says that, to tell the truth, the Dragon Quest franchise doesn't have a huge overseas market, unlike other franchises like Pokémon, which is a game more for children and which releases new games that capture young players. On the other hand, Dragon Quest has the problem of capturing new players in addition to the existing ones who are already getting older.

Finally, Okamoto-san is very excited by this news, also because he loves board games, and since he also loves the Dragon Quest franchise, it's really a collaboration with Takara Tomy that he's been anticipating for decades. Tanu-chan also wants to play this new Dragon Quest release with Okamoto-san, revealing that she used to play board games with her friends at home.
