> 【プロデューサー人生】ゲーム業界引退まであと少し。今の心境を語ります。

## --

Firstly, Okamoto-san reveals that only eight months remain before he retires on June 10th of the coming year. Moreover, he feels sad thinking about this point in his life after having worked in the video game industry for 45 or 46 years.

Okamoto-san explains that he's found that nowadays young people already do tasks like watching short videos to learn things very quickly, two or more times faster than what he can do with 1.5x. He also knows that he's gotten a lot older, and he now seems to be the studio's grandfather who also talks a lot. Furthermore, once he leaves, other people could take on his position.

Okamoto-san then emphasizes that when we put together all his videos, we'd get Okamoto-san, and by watching these videos, we can hear his thoughts as well as view his faults.

Finally, Okamoto-san advises us to share our thoughts about this topic if we have something to say as well.
