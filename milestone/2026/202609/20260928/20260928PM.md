First, Okamoto-san asks Ms. Tanu and Ms. Tapi if they've ever used a Matching App. Ms. Tanu answers no, while Ms. Tapi answers yes, the same with Okamoto-san.

Next, Okamoto-san asks Ms. Tapi if she's ever edited her photo, and she answers no, again the same with Okamoto-san, who explains that when we edit our photos, people's expections go up, and he thinks that it's a waste of time if when we finally meet, the photo and the actual person aren't the same.

Okamoto-san gives the example of when he went to meet a woman, whom he had found in a Matching App, and while the woman wrote in her profile that she's a little slim, the reality was completely different, because she was very fat.

Okamoto-san thus advises not editing our photos, nor writing false information, because doing these things seems to be the same as deceiving people, and, as a result, the original purpose of meeting a partner is lost. While Okamoto-san admits that he wants to lower his age by 20 years old, he really writes his real age, which is 65 years old.

Lastly, Okamoto-san also wants us to write our comments if there's something that we'd want to say about this topic.

I'd therefore like to say that, in the Philippines, it seems that women or young women from traditional families or those that aren't too radical want their future husband to be able to also take care of their mother or grandmother.

However, this observation that I have after working at the clinic is very challenging for the male, because the reason why the mother or the grandmother visits the clinic is that she has a health problem, which is related to lifestyle that they can't anymore change. And when we talk about lifestyle, this includes daily food as well as exercises.

For example, I think that if other people looked into what I eat, they'd find that I don't like many things including sweet beverages, so much so that it seem that life doesn't anymore have flavor. Furthermore, I have a schedule that lets me do exercises in exchange for a low salary, the latter being very important particularly for another family that doesn't exercise due to their schedule, and thus would need more money for medicine and to be able to survive.
