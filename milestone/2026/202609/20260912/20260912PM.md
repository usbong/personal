First, Okamoto-san informs us that Ms. Tapi and Ms. Tanu aren't with him in Malaysia at the moment, because both women have returned to Japan, the latter, in particular, due to an issue related to her visa.

Okamoto-san next tells us that he's a "Mayora" or a person who loves the mayonnaise. Okamoto-san also recalls that when he was poor, he used to eat by putting mayonnaise on top of the rice, while adding a little bit of soy sauce. He also admits that, of course, there are many ways to create this type of food including adding eggs as well as butter, but, this time around, what Okamoto-san would like to emphasize is the nutrition that could be obtained from this food, even though it makes people gain weight just like in his case before, especially if a person eats more mayonnaise than he can use for his daily living.

Okamoto-san also points out that he's talking about the Kewpie mayonnaise with the smoked flavor, which appears to be not yet sold in Malaysia. He says that there are actually many varieties of Kewpie including the spicy flavor. Furthermore, he clarifies that he doesn't suck the Kewpie mayonnaise at all which is produced not in jars like in jams but in a form more comfortable to get hold of it by only being squeezed. On this regard, Okamoto-san compares the mayonnaise of other countries such as the U.S. where it seems that the jars of mayonnaise are easier to open for people than what Kewpie uses. 

Okamoto-san further mentions beers and red wines, which he used to drink with Ms. Tanu but not very much, even though he knows that Ms. Tanu can drink more than she drinks. Okamoto-san says that, to tell the truth, mayonnaise doesn't necessarily cause weight gain among consumers. He points out that, actually, in the past, and for 50 years, it's been said that oil causes weight gain, but if a person uses what he gets from these food by exercising or some other activity, he shouldn't gain weight. In fact, mayonnaise doesn't have any sugar whatsoever.

In the end, Okamoto-san concludes by saying that he continues to leave the house in the morning in order to do his walking exercise. As a result, his weight is going down. However, in his most recent visit to the hospital, his doctor has given him some medications after examining his blood. Although he shares that almost all his metrics were normal, he has to take those medicines for hypertension.
