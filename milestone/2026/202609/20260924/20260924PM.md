First, Okamoto-san reveals that, to tell the truth, he had wanted to visit another place, which is in Nagoya prefecture to observe the architecture. While he had already reserved a hotel, Okamoto-san decided to cancel the reservation due to a storm, so he visited Sawara instead.

According to Okamoto-san, he's visited Sawara many times, although it's not anymore the main destination for tourists unlike before when it was said that it's better than the Edo of old. However, Okamoto-san explains that he has two reasons why he'd like to visit it again.

One is that there's a shop specialized in creating ear swabs that are custom-made based on the customer's ear. Okamoto-san further points out that each person has his own ear shape, so he wanted very much this local product.

The second reason is that there's an old inn that's been renovated and made into a hotel just like what Okamoto-san wants to create as well. In addition, they let Okamoto-san stay in a room that had been a storehouse of a businessman and has also been renovated.

Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that we can learn a lot by visiting Katori City, which may remind us of the cartographer, Tadataka Ino, also mentioning that he himself makes these visits as a form of preparation before his retirement.
