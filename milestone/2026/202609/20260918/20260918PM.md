> Stories from Game Producer Yoshiki Okamoto: The YouTube LIVE video for September 18, 2026 on what makes a person feel happy

> Writer: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-09-19

## --

Dear friends,

Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto, Ms. Tapi and Ms. Tanu's YouTube LIVE video about what makes a person feel happy.

## --

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ker5nLoqZN0; last accessed: 20260918

> ゲームプロデューサー岡本吉起のYouTubeライブ（26年9月18日）

## --

Okamoto-san begins by saying that he thinks that nobody wants to feel unhappy. He admits that he feels happy when another person feels happy.

Okamoto-san next asks both Ms. Tapi and Ms. Tanu what makes them feel happy. Ms. Tapi answers that she feels very happy when she's with her friends, especially because she is a very active fan of her favorite musicians. Ms. Tanu, for her part, reveals that she felt very happy when she went out to eat with her older brother and her younger sister after returning to Japan. She says that she thought that she had been enstranged with them and her friends, but she was very surprised to find out that they invited her to have a meal together.

Okamoto-san points out that he always wants everyone to be able to feel happy, and when even only one person feels sad, he also feels sad. He gives the example of his two children, an older daughter and a younger son. He says that when he had wanted to help his son in establishing his own business by giving him money, his son became very angry. He thinks that it's because he's male. Meanwhile, his daughter doesn't feel bad when he gives her money for food, among other things.

He also reveals that, at times, what would make us feel happy would make 70% of the people feel sad, such that Okamoto-san thinks that he won't really feel very happy as a result. On the other hand, he also admits that when we take everyone into account, we'd only probably get, in terms of percentage, 2% worth of happiness in the end.

There's still, of course, much more to say, such as the difference between Ms. Tapi and her older brother, who's a graduate of the University of Tokyo, as well as Ms. Tanu's two siblings, an older brother and an older sister, who have all previously lived with Okamoto-san in his house in Malaysia.

But before closing, I'd like to mention a little bit that, with respect to the former, Ms. Tapi recounts an incident in the past when her brother chose a scarf that she thought to have been very old-fashioned. With regard to the latter, Okamoto-san reveals that the older sister of Ms. Tanu seems to have not been able to experience a life with him as wonderful as she, who was able to wear without any issues whatsoever a one-piece swimsuit, has had.
