Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto's lecture about Apple's new product, the "iPhone Duo," which is coming out this October 23, 2026.

Okamoto-san first presents to us a table of the iPhone Duo's prices, which are differentiated by the memory that each specific model has. There are 256GB, 512GB, 1TB and 2TB memories that are priced 1,999 USD, 2,199 USD, 2,599 USD and 3,199 USD respectively. 

Okamoto-san is considering buying the 1TB or the 2TB, although he thinks that it's too expensive. In fact, he believes that this new iPhone model is too expensive for almost all Japanese, because the Japanese currency is currently valued very low. On the other hand, in the U.S., the price seems to be not too much, as though they're paying only 120,000 yen instead of 400,000 or so yen.

With respect to this point, Okamoto-san laments that Japan has become a country that has very low salaries, and recalls that, a long time ago, it was said that while Japan has the second-largest GDP in the world, it's a country where the citizens suffer greatly. As with during that time, Okamoto-san feels that foreign products have become very expensive these days.

Okamoto-san therefore asks if this new product is worth a salary of two months, and he answers no. Okamoto-san also points out that while he's a gamer and the iPhone Duo's two screens are very useful to view more information in games such as Monster Hunter NOW, he thinks that the price already reaches the Gaming PC's.

Okamoto-san then points out that the iPhone Duo is 1.5 times larger than the iPhone Pro MAX, in addition to being foldable. He's thus grateful for this improvement, especially because he'd also like to watch videos from Netflix as well as YouTube with it.

Furthermore, he says that he currently reads on the Kindle with his iPad mini, which he brings whenever he goes outside, and he thinks that if the iPhone Duo became the substitute for his iPad mini, the price difference would be big. However, if he could bring one iPhone Duo instead of his iPad mini and his other phones, he believes that it will be more convenient for him in terms of the weight as well as the charging of the battery. Also, he mentions that he likes the round corners of the iPhone Duo.

However, the features that Okamoto-san has been accustomed to like Face ID and Telephoto Lens have been abandoned in this new iPhone model, the latter he isn't sure if it's because there aren't too many users who used it or the price would go up significantly by adding the feature. Okamoto-san admits that he's very slow in getting used to using new applications including video games. But given that he brings four mobile phones whenever he leaves the house to take a walk, the idea of using only one iPhone Duo to do what he wants attracts him. 

Finally, Okamoto-san plans to buy an iPhone Duo 2TB for his work, although he doesn't recommend it to everyone, because 1TB may already be better due to the price as well as the use.
