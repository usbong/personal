Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto's lecture about "CAMP F.I.R.E.," a goal that's been created to mean striving to achieve F.I.R.E. (Financial Independence, Retire Early) temporarily in order to accomplish another objective such as studying abroad, among others.

Okamoto-san explains that he thinks that 90% of the problems that students face when they aim to study outside Japan is money. This is why Okamoto-san observes that there are people who work even during holidays. They also have a second job, invest in the stock market, as well as not spend too much in order to save money. Okamoto-san further emphasizes that foreign students generally aren't allowed to work in other countries. 

However, of course, there are people who prefer to spend all that they earn, so Okamoto-san advises having a goal like studying outside Japan. Moreover, even if the student in the end decides not to leave Japan, Okamoto-san says that this isn't bad. Okamoto-san thinks that after setting aside 18,000 yen every month and saving so much, like, for instance, 5 million yen, the student perhaps doesn't anymore want to spend his own money, and may prefer to use the money of his parents or another person's. However, he believes that the training to achieve this goal is very educational and indispensable.

Finally, Okamoto-san admits that the two things that he spends the most are for his mobile communication and in-app purchases. Further still, he wants to hear our opinions about this topic. Therefore, I'd like to respond that I agree that it's easy to spend another person's money, but it's harder to spend what one has earned and saved through work.
