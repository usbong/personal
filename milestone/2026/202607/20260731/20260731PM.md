Okamoto-san explains by giving the example of wines, saying that there are those that are sold after maturing for 25 years, and it's said that they are better for this very reason.

Okamoto-san also gives the example of pants that include those from 1995, though the better ones, he says, are those from 1960, especially Levi's jeans. Okamoto-san also points out that the pants that have small damages are better.

Next, Okamoto-san teaches that the truth is that there's another word that is "Antique" to mean old merchandise that are older than the vintage ones. However, he clarifies that the products from the Edo period are already too old, such that he thinks that "antique" cannot be used to refer to them.

Okamoto-san also recounts that there are products including pants and watches that are sold only for a limited time, such that they are more expensive after 10 or more years. Further, these vintage products can have stories such as those related to the brand or the design of the letters when the designers during that time were still trying out what they wanted. Okamoto-san then points out that these vintage products move in a "V" in terms of price with the passing of time.

Okamoto-san then shares with us that there are two places in which vintage products can be bought. One is in specialty stores, although Okamoto-san warns us that these stores sell them very expensively, because they already know their value, in addition to their business depending on the seller's ability to persuade clients, who may initially have not wanted to buy. The other place is through the auction, in which vintage products can be found very cheaply while mixed with many other products, because the seller doesn't know. However, Okamoto-san admits that it's easy to get deceived at the auction, although, on the other hand, it's very fun to find or look for these vintage products there. 

Finally, Okamoto-san answers several questions, also saying that he used to wear the uniform of an American soldier from the second world war for cosplay events.
