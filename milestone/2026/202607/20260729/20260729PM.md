> Stories from Game Producer Yoshiki Okamoto: The teachers from whom Yoshiki Okamoto has learned a lot about the video game industry

> Writer: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-07-30

## --

Dear friends,

Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto's lecture about the teachers from whom he's learned a lot about the video game industry.

## --

First, Okamoto-san points out that we respect people who are capable of achieving what we cannot, while we don't like those who aren't capable of accomplishing things even the very basic ones.

Next, Okamoto-san admits that he doesn't run very fast since before, so he's decided that it's not a demerit for him, though he encourages everyone to run. He further explains that it's the same idea why there are young people who can't do math very well. Despite this, he announces that since he's turned 65 years old already, he can now finally understand this subject better.

Okamoto-san also clarifies that he has many teachers who have taught him, pointing out that a young person in a company can learn many things from his co-workers, both bosses and subordinates, as well as clients.

In the end, Okamoto-san concludes that there are things that he can't do well, even though he strives very hard, so he greatly respects people who can do these things and from whom Okamoto-san has learned a lot. Because of this, he wants to share the lessons that he's learned to people who want to listen to him, saying that he's open to receiving inquiries from anyone interested in the design and development of video games.
