> Stories from Game Producer Yoshiki Okamoto: Mr. NISHIMURA, an ex-Capcom employee who's moved to the mountains of Nara Prefecture to develop a game

Dear friends,

Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto's lecture about Mr. NISHIMURA, an ex-Capcom employee who's moved to the mountains of Nara Prefecture in order to develop a game, while tilling the land near nature, along with the fresh air and a waterfall.

In fact, the Steam game, which is called "Veritas Tales: Witch of the Dark Castle," is a gamebook, the same or similar to the "Choose Your Own Adventure" book series with 300 drawings, a task that very much surprised Okamoto-san, RPG elements and set in a Fantasy world that is believed to revitalize people wearied with life.

Okamoto-san also recounts that the first time that they met was when Mr. NISHIMURA visited Capcom for the interview, which Okamoto-san was still conducting in those years for applicants of the studio. Moreover, Okamoto-san reveals that Mr. NISHIMURA is from the Ehime prefecture, the same as him, although he quickly clarifies that this isn't the only reason why he likes NISHIMURA, saying that Mr. NISHIMURA had studied in a school specialized in design just like him but better than the one to which Okamoto-san had gone. Furthermore, Mr. NISHIMURA also moved to Osaka City in order to look for work as designer or illustrator.

Next, Okamoto-san recalls that Mr. NISHIMURA was in-charge of work related to drawing pixels for games such as the fighting game, Cyberbots, up to Monster Hunter, his last work at Capcom, in which he had been the chief background designer. Afterward, Mr. NISHIMURA transferred to Vanillaware, another studio wherein Mr. George Kamitani is currently the chief of design, who, according to Okamoto-san's explanation, left his former studio, because he says that there were already heads more capabable than him.

Finally, Okamoto-san encourages us to also try out Mr. NISHIMURA's game on Steam.
