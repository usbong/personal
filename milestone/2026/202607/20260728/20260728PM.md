Okamoto-san recounts that this new release promotes EVs with 100 color varieties of trucks with very cheap prices at 4 million yen instead of its original price of 8 million. In addition, while Tesla sells high-class EVs, the "Slate Auto" appears to be for buyers who already own a car, especially because surfboards and other big objects can be put at the back of the truck. Okamoto-san also announces that when reservations were opened for this EV, it already had 10,000 people who've placed a reservation.

Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that although this new series of EV isn't yet sold in Japan, he believes that it'll surely be able to sell in the U.S., especially given the country's geography with long roads, among other things.
