Okamoto-san begins the talk by asking Tanu-chan how much money she wants her future husband to make each year. She answers that she wants a very high amount, because she wants their family when they marry to be able to have enough money to travel as well as send their children to university, among other things.

Okamoto-san then comments that while 10 years ago, a man's income of 300,000 yen each month was enough, he thinks that this isn't anymore so today. He thinks that it's now 500,000 yen each month to accumulate 5 million yen and more every year. However, he emphasizes that after hearing Tanu-chan's reasons for wanting so much money, he thinks that 8 million yen every year would be the correct amount for the income.

Still, Okamoto-san points out that only less than 10% of Japanese people earn so much. This is why he suggests achieving it through being a power couple (a couple who don't have children) in which the husband and his spouse both work and earn money whose total amounts to 8 million yen each year. For example, the husband earns 500,000 each month, while the spouse earns 300,000 each month.

Okamoto-san also explains that a man who earns 8 million yen each year by himself probably prefers a women who earns a lot as well. But if the woman lowers her requirement, she'd surely meet a man who earns 5 million yen each year.

Furthermore, given that Tanu-chan is 24 years old, if she continues to invest in the stock market, she could get enough money to be able to marry a man who also has a lot of money.

Okamoto-san emphasizes that if both invested in the stock market, in 5 years, 1 million yen each year would grow up to 3 million.

In the end, Okamoto-san concludes that he advises everyone to invest as early as possible.
