> I.T. Pro Stories: Why Consistently Tardy People Don't Pay Their Debts

> Writer: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-11-10

## --

Dear friends,

Today I'd like to share with you the reason why consistently tardy people don't pay their debts.

First and foremost, this behavior of arriving late has become so commonplace even at the clinic and among the people whom I happen to interact with that I thought I'd better bring your attention to it as well.

Besides the excuse that it's better to arrive late than never, something my classmates and I were taught back in high school, there's also one about arriving exactly on time. While the latter may sound hardly innocuous, you'll later find out that that same person would gradually arrive tardier and tardier.

I doubt that he ever pays his own bills, and could even be trusted to have a credit card, because every payment due missed increases the amount of credit card debt that the person would have to pay. 

The age-old German addage, "Zeit ist Geld (Time is Gold)" should ring a bell. If it doesn't, anyone who's lent that person money shouldn't anymore expect to be paid back the full amount. In fact, many of these same people borrow money without the intention of paying back, waiting for the lender to die and thus get relieved from that portion of their entire debt, which still continues to rise.

Moreover, thanks to information technology, we now know that some of the staff at the clinic need at least an additional 6,000 PHP every month.

Before closing, let me share with you that my grade school classmates who regularly came in late were eventually kicked out once we entered high school. 

It is a lesson that all grade school students ought to have learned by the time they reach high school. My classmates and their parents needed a wake up call that something wasn't right and they needed to address it or else. 

Lastly, among these tardy classmates of mine included those who chopped off the heads of dragonflies because he could, as well as stole a fire extinguisher. If students are allowed to graduate from grade school without learning the value of punctuality, then it shouldn't come as a big surprise that many so-called grownups cannot pay their debts, nor be expected to fulfill their jobs.

This is all. 

Thanks very much.

Regards,

Mike
