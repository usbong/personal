First, Okamoto-san asks both Ms. Tapi and Ms. Tanu about what they think is the flower that best represents the Japanese woman.
 
Ms. Tapi and Ms. Tanu both agree that it's surely the sunflower, which symbolizes a cheerful and open personality.

Okamoto-san, for his part, reveals that while it seems that people have the image of a very beautiful woman with white skin, he thinks that, previously, it was more like Ms. Tanu, who although measures 170 cm in height, doesn't have so white a skin. She also doesn't have small buttocks. Thus, he thinks that the Suzuran from the Lily flower family might be the most apt. 

On the other hand, the flower Sakura evokes the image of men, particularly the samurais. With regard to this point, he recalls the book, Sangokushi (The Romance of the Three Kingdoms), in which many factions fought each other, and amid the turmoil brought about by the series of wars, the group that carried out the coup d'etat is justified by winning, while the other group becomes the one who is bad for having been defeated.

As it is, Okamoto-san attributes Japan's current economic problem to the change in the mentality of the Japanese people about work, pointing out that, formerly, in the period decades prior, the bosses made the young people work very hard; however, this isn't anymore so today, because they've become more lenient. He thus bemoans the reality that when he left CAPCOM, there were people who, thinking that he was still at the studio, would say that he didn't anymore lead his team very well.

Afterward, in the next part of the video, Okamoto-san shares with us that he continues to take walks every morning as part of his exercise, which his medical doctor for diabetes had advised him. He admits that he only needs 30 minutes of walking, but because he doesn't like only half an hour, he's opted to walk longer. In fact, this morning, he says that he's walked 5 hours. Moreover, he does muscle training exercises, although he still hasn't been able to do all the 10 sets.

He further says that Ms. Tanu used to accompany him when she was still in Malaysia, even at three o'clock in the morning, so he invites Ms. Tanu to do this exercises with him again this January if she wants to come back to Malaysia from Japan. Meanwhile, he wants Ms. Tapi who's returned to Malaysia to accompany him.

Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that he's brought down his weight, and the last time that he visited his medical doctor, he informed Okamoto-san that his blood sugar level is okay. Furthermore, he's grateful to the exercises as well as the other regimen that he does, which is to manage the amount of food that he eats.

This is all. There are, of course, many more things to say, but I should already stop here.
