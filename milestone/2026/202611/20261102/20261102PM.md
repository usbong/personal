> Stories from Game Producer Yoshiki Okamoto: Ms. Tanu's request about eating breakfast at the Saizeriya restaurant in Maihama, Chiba Prefecture

> Writer: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-11-03

## --

Dear friends,

Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto and Ms. Tani's conversation about the latter's request to Okamoto-san about eating breakfast at the Saizeriya restaurant located in front of the Maihama Train Station in Chiba Prefecture.

Okamoto-san says that he knows the place, because it's the very train station where visitors get off to arrive in Disneyland, though he admits that he's not eaten there yet at all.

Ms. Tanu shares that the restaurant is on the first floor of the Dream Gate Hotel. He also points out that, previously, only the clients of the hotel could eat the buffet at the restaurant, though the management has already opened it up to the public. Okamoto-san responds that it's probably because the business isn't doing well, and then asks Ms. Tanu what food they could eat there.

Ms. Tanu replies that the restaurant only offers pastas, pizzas, and also the Milano-style Doria that she very much likes to eat. Meanwhile, the entire buffet costs 1,300 yen per person. Afterward, she adds that according to the reviews, there's also the croissant that isn't included in Saizeriya's general menu. However, Okamoto-san thinks that it's not too economical if they would have to go to Maihama only to eat it.

Okamoto-san recalls having eaten at Saizeriya with Ms. Tapi and the boss of a company that they were working with, spending as much as 17,000 yen all in all. Okamoto-san explains that he wanted to try the wine, which, it's said, a woman who likes it is the best, although Okamoto-san doubted it. Still, Ms. Tapi and the boss liked the wine, even though Okamoto-san disagreed.

Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that the food at Saizeriya isn't bad, although the wine is questionable. Nonetheless, he decides to go with Ms. Tanu and two other people to the restaurant to eat at the buffet next time.
