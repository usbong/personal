This time, Leo speaks to us about his life after graduating from university. We may recall that he's told us that he participated in several Virtua Fighter 2 tournaments, and, in fact, admits that he played a lot when he was in university, so much so that he wanted to learn more how he could contribute to the community. Because of this, he entered a company to help people with disabilities. 

He also recounts that he wanted to provide another perspective toward common ideas that people have. For example, he points out that misconceptions occur when people use words, so he thought about expressing the meaning that we want to convey through dancing. For this reason, he used the money of several milliones of yen that he had saved from work to study ballet in France for a little over a year. He also reveals that his mom and younger sister themselves did ballet, and because Leo also wanted to exercise more, he left for France.

He next admits that he didn't have any skill in dancing, and even worse, he was already in his 20s. He gives the example of stretching in which he couldn't move his body as well as he had thought. With respect to this point, he thought that the common way to stretch was probably wrong. He then wondered about the reasons as to why we have to expand our body, as well as why we become stiff.

He explains that what we generally know about stretching isn't always applicable to everyone, so he researched about this topic. For example, our body becomes rigid when it's cold or when we don't move at all. Furthermore, he reveals that in these cases people don't have any reason to move themselves or become warmer. He also shares that what he thought is the reason why our body becomes stiff, saying that it's because we're afraid to do a task like stretching.
