Dear friends,

Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto's lecture about the news that Manga Artist Tetsuya Chiba, who's received the national award for his contribution to Japanese culture, has just passed away at 87 years old on October 22nd.

Okamoto-san recounts that news about the death of Chiba-san came out after only a week since Shigeru Miyamoto received the same award. Okamoto-san points out that both Japanese have contributed to Japanese culture, especially because for a long time, Japanese people still considered both manga comics and video games as distractions to the studies of students. However, decades later, this isn't necessarily so anymore.

Okamoto-san thanks Master Chiba for writing stores in which the protagonists who, although aren't super heroes, could improve themselves by striving. As a result, readers of his manga comics, which include "Ashita no Joe (Tomorrow's Joe)," realized that Japan is a magnificent country.

Okamoto-san also gives another manga comic called "Tenki ni nare (Become the Weather!)" in which Okamoto-san emphasizes that while he doesn't often play golf nor as much, he thinks that a reader of the manga comic could use its lessons, because it's really a very natural thing to do them.

Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that he thanks Chiba-san for his contribution to Japanese culture, and wants Japan to be able to continue to strive in order to become better and stronger.
