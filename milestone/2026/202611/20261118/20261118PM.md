## --

First, Okamoto-san explains that, in general, our face has the shape of our parents, and when we pass 40 years old, we would be able to see some changes with respect to our expressions due to our experiences, both positive and negative.

Therefore, Okamoto-san points out that a person, who doesn't live in a positive way, also wouldn't have a positive face. Furthermore, Okamoto-san admits that while he wanted so much to do physical exercises, due to his lifestyle related to his work, after 38 years old, he hadn't been able to put more time to do physical exercises for almost 30 years. However, he points out that another person like Masahiro Sakurai could do exercises using a stationary bike, while he works on debugging tasks, something very amazing.

For his part, given that he's 65 years old, he has strived hard to reduce his weight. Also, while he would really like to go out and drink beer, he reveals that, unfortunately, he ends up going out without drinking any beer at all. He also emphasizes that the liver is the part of our body in-charge of the blood, so it's a very vital component that affects our health.

He next reveals that nowadays many people even his own mom, who's 88 years old, are worried if he, who's 65 years old, is all right with respect to his health or not, because he's reduced his weight so much by taking walks every morning for up to 10 km. However, he emphasizes that he still has the goal of reducing his weight down to 64.9 kg.

In summary, he advises us to change our life as early as possible, so that we won't end up failing like him.

This is all. Of course, there are many more things to say, but I'll already need to stop here. Okamoto-san also wants to read our comments if we have any.
