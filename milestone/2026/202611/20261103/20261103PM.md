He says that he's gotten information about the new console called "Project Helix," which will be out in 2028. In addition, it's foreseen that it'll have a price of around 190,000 to 280,000 yen. That is, the console will cost a lot, given that a Gaming PC costs 80,000 yen these days. As with the case of the Gaming PC, the increase is due to the high prices of goods including the parts necessary to create computers. As it is, Microsoft's new console will be among the computers with high specs.

Okamoto-san also thinks that as its price is also quite high, only the developers who believe that they'll be able to make money are going to developer for this console. On the other hand, he also thinks that, perhaps, there would be a discount in the price if the consumer becomes a monthly subscriber of XBOX's online service, in which he could play the games from Microsoft's library. Because of this, it's possible that he'd only have to pay 150,000 yen for the console.

This is all.

Before closing, because Okamoto-san also wants to read our comments about this topic, I'd like to say that Microsoft's console has always attracted me since a long time ago, though I've never bought one due to the price, in exchange for what I could play or do with it. I think that since the release of the first XBOX, Microsoft's goal has been to attract players including video game developers to buy computers that have been installed with Windows. I also recall in the GameFan magazine decades ago the announcement that SEGA's Dreamcast console would have Windows as its operating system. Afterward, maybe because the Dreamcast couldn't make as much money as expected, Microsoft would release its own console, the XBOX.
