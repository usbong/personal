In this part of their video, Leo recounts that because he couldn't stand very well to do the stretching exercises in ballet, he began to study the body's movements including the fingers of the hand. Furthermore, he says that he could do this for seven hours without stop. Also, he's continued to do this since seven years ago thanks to his ability to concentrate. Because of this, he's learned that when the body moves toward the direction that it likes, it becomes more relaxed as a result. He then points out that our body's stiffness isn't a muscular problem but one of the mind.

He next reveals that his mom suffered from hip pain, so Leo wanted to help her. Thus, he entered a job related to the body like physical therapy or sports training, although he admits that he's not received any formal training. With respect to this point, Okamoto agrees that Leo's words sound suspicious, but after paying him, he confirms that his own hip problem doesn't anymore cause him any pain at the moment, and he firmly believes that Leo's method will finally cure it.

Leo, for his part, clarifies that they've only done 60% of the exercise, and he emphasizes that he teaches with explanations, so that his clients like Okamoto-san could understand and accept his teachings. Moreover, while he hasn't written any publication about his method at all, he reveals that he's shared in his own channel the video about this topic, and it has garnered over a million views.
