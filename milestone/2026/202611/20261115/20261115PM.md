First, Okamoto-san introduces us to Leo, who had won a series of fights counting 80 or more times using ZANGIEF against his opponents who came to SHATOU EX Game Center in Kyobashi.

Leo recounts that he was still in high school and was 16 years old when he played the test version of the new entry to the Street Fighter II series when the game developer was testing it at the game center for their "location testing," recalling that his older brother had told him about it.

He also reveals that he had already played the prior entries of the series, and he loved to use ZANGIEF, not only because the use of both RYU and KEN already gave the user a lot of power, but also because he liked to use ZANGIEF's attack called SPINNING PILE DRIVER. Furthermore, he emphasizes that he discovered a way to enter the commands very efficiently, so much so that before the update, he could win against anyone easily.

He explains that after looking into it, he found that given that he could enter a punch command by releasing the button that he had pressed, he could then press the weakest button first to punch quickly, execute the directional commands with the joystick, and when he was in the command for jump, he'd press the stronger punch button in the middle to launch the attack. We may recall that pressing the first button is important, so that ZANGIEF wouldn't jump.
  
With respect to this, he points out that, in general, users had to jump first before executing the SPINNING PILE DRIVER, so the method that he was using was very powerful, especially because the damage of this attack had also been quite lot at the time.

Despite the change in the damage, Leo reveals to us that he could still win, because in those days many players used RYU or KEN, meaning that he could execute the commands more quickly than the animation frames of the HADOKEN, in addition to the fact that the said attack pulls the opponent closer to ZANGIEF. On the other hand, he admits that another character like HONDA would give him a lot more challenge, although not many people used him.

Finally, Okamoto-san, for his part, emphasizes that the use of releasing the button had already existed in the first STREET FIGHTER game at the game center. Moreover, he reveals that the execution of the commands would actually depend on the stage, which could influence the animation frame.
