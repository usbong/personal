Okamoto-san admits that he's experienced setbacks in his life, particularly when he was heading his own studio, Game Republic, and given that his client couldn't anymore pay them the remaining three months, he, for his part, also couldn't pay his workers. While Okamoto-san also wanted the court to do something to help them, in the end, it didn't amount to anything. Furthermore, although Okamoto-san wanted to at least finish the project, they released a product that got bad reviews.

Okamoto-san also teaches that life is such in that while we cannot accomplish what we want, we should think about the reason for the outcome, saying that the goal may be to lower our weight or some other, but the important thing is that we strive, because a good outcome always comes afterward.

Finally, Okamoto-san reminds us that in order to do things well, we must never forget to love ourselves.
