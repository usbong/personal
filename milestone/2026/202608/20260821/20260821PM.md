First, Okamoto-san shares with us that three women have visited him in his house in Malaysia. They've graduated from a university exclusively for women and have asked him what book he would recommend them. Okamoto-san answered by giving them the title of Ikenami-san's book, which was released in the 70s and is called "kenkyaku shobai ("The Swordsman's Trade")," a book that is actually a series of a total of 20 books with several stories starred by a man, Kohei Akiyama, who is 59 years old and is a skilled swordsman with his wife, Oharu, who is 19 years old during the time of old Okitsugu Tanuma. Furthermore, the married couple have a son named Daijiro Akiyama, who's rather big in size, and trains as a swordsman as well. Further still, Old Tanuma has a daughter who wears men's clothing and is called Mifuyu Sasaki with whom Akiyama-san's son would marry. However, this pair always have difficulty meeting each other to be able to talk due to the situation where they're in, although it appears that they already feel in-love with each other.

In summary, Okamoto-san reveals that it's a book that was serialized when Okamoto-san was still in elementary school. He also admits that although he didn't own a big enough storage to keep many books during that time, such that he would give a book to a friend every time he finished one, he thinks that it's better to keep these books, in particular, and read them at differente ages in order to better understand the situation of each protagonist. Finally, it's a series that already has a conclusion unlike other series, and Okamoto-san thinks that a person would need half a year to finish the whole thing.
