According to Okamoto-san, Saudi Arabia is building the park costing 7 billion USD in the suburbs of Paris and which will have a land area of 500,000 square meters, comparable with the Disneyland in Tokyo, which has an area of about 510,000 square meters.

Okamoto-san thinks that it will take 10 years to complete the big project, which, it's said, is one that the developers would have wanted to show to Mr. Akira Toriyama, the creator of Dragon Ball and who has already passed away even before its date of completion. 

In summary, while Okamoto-san is very excited about this announcement, he's also a bit sad, because the park isn't being built in Japan, where the franchise had, in fact, been created.
