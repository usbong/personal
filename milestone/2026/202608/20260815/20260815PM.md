Okamoto-san tells us that in Japan, until May of this year, 180 cases of disasters related to fire have occurred. In addition, these batteries that take on the form of powerbanks aren't anymore allowed to be used inside the airplane, including plugging a mobile phone into them. Therefore, passengers should put them inside the bag without using them at all while in the plane. 

Okamoto-san advises us that given these incidents, it's better to be cautious by buying a more expensive, but safe-to-use battery. Further, he teaches that a battery without any brand may be cheap, but it's not worth buying if it catches fire. He also points out that there's the PSE seal to signify that a battery has been checked, such that the batteries that don't have this brand aren't advisable to use.

Finally, Okamoto-san advises us to buy batteries from a brand that we can trust.
