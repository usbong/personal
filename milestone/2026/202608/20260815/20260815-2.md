[00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O7LAb0RT9Sw) I.T. Pro shares in Japanese the news that registration for the upcoming Game Off 2026 programming tournament is now open. He admits that while he's been joining since 2021, he's never been in the top 10; however, he points out that making money out of winning the tournament isn't a sure outcome, and he prefers to find another worthwhile reason for joining, such as creating a gift for family and friends for the coming Christmas season.

[05:07](https://youtu.be/O7LAb0RT9Sw?si=_fRBYyYHRZBtoXgn&t=307) I.T. Pro talks about what game he's thinking about creating, although he can't be too specific yet since the theme for this year hasn't yet been revealed, which is why he's been improving his DIY engine in order to be able to do many things by adding elements used in side-scrolling and beat 'em up games. While he'd want to create something that makes the player think, he isn't planning on making a game that has mazes or labyrinths. Finally, he believes that his team's Game Off entry last year was better than their prior submissions, although it was still too difficult according to feedback from family and friends, and he attributes this to programmers and debuggers wanting something a lot more challenging after playing the game's prototype so many times.
