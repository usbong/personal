Okamoto-san begins by saying that the current salary at well-known studios has risen to 300,000 yen per month, an amount that is 10% to 20% the average. However, the situation isn't relaxed, because employees must work a lot for a goal that they cannot achieve elsewhere.

Okamoto-san next admits that before leaving Capcom, he was earning 70 million yen per year as an executive. He also mentions that after leaving, the employees that remained received 10,000 yen less each year in income. Still, as Okamoto-san says, in reality, the amount that he received as executive wasn't enough, because he also had to pay for food expenses when he went out with his team, instead of leaving them to pay for their own meals, and so on.

Okamoto-san also points out that each time the salary increases, the employee begins to want better things in terms of quality such as housing, cars, and clothing. At this point, Okamoto-san cites the "Parkinson's 2nd Law," which says that expenses go up to meet the amount of income that the person makes. This is why employees at video game studios seem to never be able to achieve FIRE. They also don't have the time to look for other ways to make money, aside from investing in the S&P.

Okamoto-san believes that while the decision to leave a studio in order to achieve FIRE would, of course, depend on the person, Okamoto-san reveals that his earnings after his time at Capcom, grew 100 times. Nonetheless, when he himself was managing his own studio, he strived to give his employees more money including the opportunity to be able to achieve FIRE. Still, Okamoto-san believes that young people have to strive harder and experience difficulties with less money, because he thinks that they should work to achieve something that isn't measured only in terms of money.
 
Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that the ideal is to work without worrying about money after having achieved FIRE, such that what the person receives as income is only extra. Therefore, Okamoto-san encouarges everyone to go for the best path available to be able to achieve FIRE.
