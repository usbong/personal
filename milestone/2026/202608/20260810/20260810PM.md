> Stories from Game Producer Yoshiki Okamoto: An indie game developer no longer wants to localize the text of his game into Arabic after attempting to do so

> Writer: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-08-11

## --

Dear friends,

Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto's lecture about a post on social media that said that the author, an indie game developer, no longer wants to localize into Arabic the text in his game after attempting to do so.

## --

First, Okamoto-san points out that the Arabic language is used in the UAE as well as some of the countries in North Africa, amounting to as much as 400 million people, a number that is almost four times the population of Japan.

Next, Okamoto-san admits that, to tell the truth, it's very rare to meet a person who's capable of the Japanese language as well as Arabic. Also, in addition to the fact that the way of writing in Arabic is from right to left, there's the cultural and religious aspects that, he thinks, aren't also easy to translate or interpret, making it difficult to correct errors. Furthermore, about 50% of the people who speak Arabic are capable of speaking English as well. For these reasons, it seems that it isn't worthwhile to localize a game for players who use Arabic.

Nonetheless, Okamoto-san points out that, on the other hand, AI is already helping him a lot understand text from the German language to Japanese, so maybe it's possible to do this in this case as well.

Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that after working in the video game industry for many years and having been able to localize games in five or seven languages, he's never done localization into Arabic yet, and he thinks that the task would be very difficult to pull off as more languages are added, although he also admits that AI can help people who may want to do it.
