> Writer: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-08-09

Dear friends,

Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto's video with Mr. Makoto Ishii, an ex-Capcom employee who had also been Okamoto-san's junior employee when he was still leading projects at the studio.

First, Ishii-san introduces himself by saying that he now works at Bandai Namco in Malaysia after his time at Capcom. Moreover, he recounts that he was in-charge of tasks related to doing the pixel art of the characters from Capcom's fighting games such as Street Fighter Zero when he was still in his 20s. He also recalls that Okamoto-san was his boss who regularly visited the cubicles on their floor, such that whenever Ishii-san heard that Okamoto-san was coming, he would quickly prepare drawings that he thought Okamoto-san would like. 
He explains that Okamoto-san was very strict in completing projects exactly on schedule, and didn't like his workers not striving very hard. Because of this, they had to work everyday even during Saturdays and Sundays in order to finish the project. 

Furthermore, Ishii-san mentions that there was a waiver form that workers, who wanted to work without receiving any payment for overtime, had to sign. Okamoto-san clarifies that it didn't come from him, though he knows that the motivation that applicants had for entering the studio was primarily to create games, and he's observed that workers who could endure the situation despite not having very high skill levels were able to last longer just like him.

Therefore, they came up with ways to do the tasks quickly. For example, they looked for housing near the studio, so that they could go to the office on a bicycle and be able to work continuously. Ishii-san also recalls that Okamoto-san's bicycle was a "Slash" model, which Okamoto-san confirms to have been one that he had spent money on to buy.

Despite the busy schedule, Ishii-san admits that Okamoto-san would invite he and his young co-workers to eat Yakiniku (grilled meat) or Gyudon (meat with rice) at the end of the week like Friday in order to work the following day and strive to finish the project the coming Monday. They only returned to the house on Sunday to wash clothes. Ishii-san also mentions that Okamoto-san enjoyed very much playing sports like softball, which he would do every noontime and would invite them to play with him once or twice a month.

Okamoto-san, for his part, emphasizes the importance of development tools to be able to do tasks more quickly. Furthermore, he recalls the instance when his own boss at Capcom asked him to create the first game in the Street Fighter Zero series for the CP System 1 arcade machine, even though the next CP System 2, a better one, would already be released. What's more, after finishing the project, Okamoto-san recounts his boss telling him that the game would be for the CP System 2 in the end.

In summary, Okamoto-san and Ishii-san strived hard while they were at Capcom, so much so that they're very happy with the studio's growth throughout the time when they were working there. Okamoto-san also emphasizes that young people should strive very hard to achieve success, and he hopes that Capcom's current employees, including their colleagues who still remain there, could strive more to raise the studio's status.
