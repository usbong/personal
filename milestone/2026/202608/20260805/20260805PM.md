First, Okamoto-san tells us that some visitors have visited him in his house in Malaysia, and they wanted Okamoto-san to teach them how to play CATAN.

Okamoto-san reveals that he thinks that it was difficult for the new players to remember the rules of the game, although Okamoto-san points out that he, for his part, was able to do it well the first time. Regarding this point, Okamoto-san admits that this game has truly fascinated him very much, which is why he wants to share with us what he thinks is the strategy to win in it.

Okamoto-san says that when players begin to play CATAN, each one chooses a space to occupy. Among several spaces, the two most important in the game are the house and the road. In addition, when a player chooses a space, another player can no longer take it, such that less and less spaces become available as time goes by, thus, the player who's taken the best spaces wins.

However, a player, who seems to be winning, would be targeted by the other players. For this reason, he believes that the third player in rank appears to have a higher winning rate than the rest.

Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that the best CATAN games are when more, instead of less, possibilities open up for players to occupy spaces, such that he advises working toward accomplishing this goal through negotiation or some other means.
