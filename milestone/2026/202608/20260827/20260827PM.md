> Stories from Game Producer Yoshiki Okamoto: Recommendations to improve the Monster Hunter NOW mobile game

> Writer: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-08-28

Dear friends,

Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto's lecture about his recommendations to improve the Monster Hunter NOW mobile game.

> 【改善案７つ】モンスターハンターNow新体制の運営にココを見直してほしい！

## --

First, Okamoto-san announces that Niantic, which is well-known for mobile games like Pokémon GO, Pikmin and Monster Hunter NOW, has changed its name after getting acquired, and is now called Scopely Explore.

Okamoto-san next enumerates seven recommendations from an old man like himself who's now 65 years old, so that players of Monster Hunter NOW could enjoy the game even more. Among these recommendations, Okamoto-san points out one that is related to the idea that given that he pays money inside the game, he's accumulated too many game items, amounting to 15,000 units, so much so that he's thinking about a way that could be added by the developers to allow him to give these items to his friends. For example, there may be a competition among players, and the winner could get the item as a prize, instead of only giving these items to his friends. Also, in the latter's case, Okamoto-san says that it may be better if there's a limit to the quantity that can be given to another player.

Furthermore, Okamoto-san explains that these items such as one to recover from damages and which cost a lot for neophites are already included when they buy another item that they'd want. However, there are things like the hagitori knife that he thinks aren't enough in terms of the quanitity available within the game. In short, Okamoto-san highlights that, in the first place, he points out these points, because he wants young people to be able to play Monster Hunter NOW as well.

Finally, Okamoto-san talks about the improvements that he advises, so that there may be more players who'd continue to play Monster Hunter NOW without worrying or being too stressed out while playing in the game as well as outside it.
