## --

To start off, Okamoto-san reintroduces us the indie game, Meccha Camereon, which has been created by two developers and after only two months has already sold 20 million copies. Okamoto-san says that given that each copy costs 800 yen, each developer has thus earned 8 billion yen. Of course, we'll have to take into account taxes, among other things, which they should also pay, but Okamoot-san emphasizes that it's a magnificent achievement in which two developers were able to achieve after only two months.

Okamoto-san also points out that other games on Steam that have achieved this figure include titles like Elden Ring, PUBG and Baldur's Gate. This is why it's really a very amazing news that an indie game was also able to sell so much, especially for aspiring indie game developers.

Okamoto-san next introduces us another game from 10 years ago  called Stardew Valley, one from the slow-life genre that is set in a farm which the protagonist rebuilds while communicating with the townspeople, going to the cave to hunt monsters, and many more. The game has been created by only one person, although the main developer has brought in other members to help him further improve the game, which continues to receive updates. Also, in this case, the game costs 1,480 yen, a fairly inexpensive price according to Okamoto-san.

Finally, Okamoto-san is very excited by these two games and the news that an indie game can achieve such great success, reiterating his desire to also enter the field of indie game development.
