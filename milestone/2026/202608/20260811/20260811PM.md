First, Okamoto-san shares that he would like to travel to Thailand again this year despite the news saying that the country is losing as a tourist destination. He explains that he's just traveled to Thailand in December last year, and stayed there for two weeks. This time, he's thinking about staying for three weeks or more. In addition, he recounts that he's traveled to Thailand for work during his time at Capcom, recalling the story from his young American co-worker, who had told him that he went to Thailand for pleasure with his family, and found that the expenses were quite cheap.

However, after 10 or 20 years, it seems that Thailand has become a very expensive country, where a seafood meal cost Okamoto-san up to 5,000 yen in his most recent trip, in addition to the sceneries not being able to compare any longer with those in Okinawa in terms of beauty.

Despite these changes, Okamoto-san points out the decline in the value of the Japanese Yen as a reason for the rise in the prices, and also admits that he thinks that Thai food with its not-too-spicy curry, among others, is more delicious than Vietnamese food, although the latter includes some that mix with French cuisine. With respect to the sceneries or the tourist destinations, Okamoto-san says that visitors can meet pole dancing women to whom they can give money, a very interesting experience for those who like to go out at night.

Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that he wants to return to Thailand despite the negative points that he's given, and would like to hear our comments about this topic as well.
