First, Okamoto-san admits that he hasn't played the game yet, because while he was planning to play it with Ms. Tapi on Switch 2, he couldn't. In addition, some five clients have visited him at his house in Malaysia, such that he had to prepare meals, among other things. Therefore, he thinks that he would be able to finally play the following day, given that it's a game that has been created with big names such as Mr. Yuji Horii and Mr. Muneyuki Kaneshiro, the original creator of the manga comic book series Blue Lock, in addition to the graphics that remind him of the Momotetsu game, which Okamoto-san had been playing with up to four people previously.

With respect to the story, Okamoto-san recounts that the protagonist has died and is saving the currency called Karma in the spiritual world before his reincarnation. Also, up to four people can play it, although Okamoto-san still prefers to play with humans instead of NPCs for two hours, because he admits that the experience would be very different.

Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that he'll surely play the new release from Horii-san and Kaneshiro-san's team soon, although he points out that it appears that there aren't still many reactions on social media like X, nor videos about this game, but maybe it's still because it's really very new.
