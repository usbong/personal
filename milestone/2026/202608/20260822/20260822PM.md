This time around, Okamoto-san reveals that he's begun to take the steps that he thinks are necessary in order to realize this objective. Okamoto-san says that he's currently designing a house that has an "American House Taste" together with a colleague, who's letting him design a one-room house.

Okamoto-san explains that among his other dreams such as becoming a board game creator, this, he believes, is the most viable for him right now. Furthermore, he tells us that a house with this style would be for surfers and would have a garage in addition to a space to cook barbecues. He's also thinking about the bathroom as well as the place where he'd want to put the window.

Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that, before retiring, he's already begun to strive to realize this dream of his, and in a very relaxed way. Having said this, he also wants to read our positive comments about this update in his life.
