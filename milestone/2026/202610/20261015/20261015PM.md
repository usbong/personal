Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto's lecture five years ago about the time when he was still very young and was working at Konami.

Okamoto-san recounts that when he started at Konami, the studio had three departments: One was the headquarters to which Okamoto-san belonged, another was the factory, and the last was the development division, which, for some reason or another that he couldn't quite understand, he went to for work. 

Okamoto-san also says that while he had graduated from a design school, he learned a lot about the use of computers to do his work. He points out that the amount of work would always be much more than what could be finished given the time, so he had to do overtime (OT), so much so that he recalls an instance when he worked for two weeks even the weekends without taking a break.

During those days, Okamoto-san shares that while his room was very near the office and which he could get to in only three or five minutes, he remained at the office to continue working. He also used the container of a cup ramen noodle to bathe. Then when his boss finally allowed him to return to the house to wash his clothes, Okamoto-san only washed them without eating dinner for that evening, and didn't eat breakfast during the morning of the following day, because he had to already return to the office right away.

With respect to this, Okamoto-san admits that it may seem that he's complaining, but he says that he worked very happily at Konami, also recalling having eaten simple foods like katsudon and soba. Also, while he was only at the studio for a year and a half, nowadays, other people would regard him highly when they find out that he had worked there as well as Capcom, another great studio.

In the end, Okamoto-san concludes that he is the Okamoto of today thanks to his experiences at Konami, Capcom and more, and he's very grateful to these studios where he's also learned a lot.
