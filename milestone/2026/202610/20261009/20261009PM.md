Okamoto-san begins his talk by first admiting that he wears glasses especially when he records videos just like this time due to his age. He explains that, as a 65-year-old senior, he can no longer see well at a distance of one meter, something very different from the idea that the elderly can see things better from afar.

Okamoto-san next shares that an ex-employee of Apple is launching in Japan the new console "Nex Playground," which will use gestures like what Microsoft Kinect or Nintendo's Wii did previously. While Okamoto-san has gotten used to using controllers, he's very excited about this news, and wants to buy and try out this "Nex Playground," which will cost about 50,000 yen.

Afterward, Okamoto-san answers the questions from his viewers, and I'd like to highlight some of his answers such as the one about the legendary figure Mr. Shigeru Miyamoto whom he respects very much, also saying that while he seems to have a fun personality, he's surely very strict with respect to work, because Okamoto-san believes that if not, he wouldn't be able to release a series of masterpieces like the Legend of Zelda in which Miyamoto-san designed the parts that Okamoto-san disliked to be a lot more fun. Okamoto-san also recounts his own history of career advancement in Capcom until becoming a member of the Board of Directors 30 years ago, emphasizing the importance of rising gradually together with the company's growth throughout the years. Finally, Okamoto-san says that he's, of course, very grateful to his parents who had divorced when he was only 3 years old, and his mom again married with another spouse whom she also divorced, because despite everything, all his parents have taught him about life.
