First, Ms. Tanu asks Okamoto-san several questions about his experience visiting Junglia in Okinawa. For example, one question is related to Okamoto-san's first impression after visiting the place, in which he then replies that he went to Junglia with Ms. Tapi in November (of last year), instead of July, because they thought that summer in Japan would be very hot, so Okamoto-san chose to visit in November.

Okamoto-san also recounts that they were able to enjoy the rides by paying a fee, instead of waiting for an hour or so in the line, something that, he explains, he did, because time is more valuable for him than money. However, he thinks that it's better to go to Junglia with a romantic partner, instead of a sempai and kohai-type of colleagues at work, because, honestly speaking, there weren't a lot of rides that were really fun according to Okamoto-san, although maybe this won't necessarily be so for a couple.

Okamoto-san next talks about the food, which he enjoyed very much. It was ribs with beer that Okamoto-san even ordered a second time, because it was so delicious. Afterward, for dinner, they left Junglia to eat at a Shabu-Shabu restaurant.

Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that he's very satisfied for having visited Junglia, giving it a satisfaction rating of 70% to 80%. Also, he explains that there were few things that he didn't like, although, on the other hand, there were also few amazing things that he liked. Okamoto-san then points out that there aren't many things that visitors to Okinawa can do, and he thinks that three days may already be enough, although when talking about Okinawa, he would want to stay for one week, which would include a visit to Junglia.

With respect to the loss of 17.3 billion yen, he thinks that it was probably because management would let in too many visitors, so much so that the lines to the rides, particularly for the most popular ones, became too long, in addition to the heat.

This is all. Of course, there are many more things to tell, but I should already stop here.
