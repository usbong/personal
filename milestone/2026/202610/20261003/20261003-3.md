> I.T. Pro Stories: Our Bright Future: Why People Stand in Front of Walls

> Writer: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-10-03

## --

Dear friends,

Today I'd like to share with you the reason why people stand in front of walls.

Let me start by sharing that when I was in fifth or sixth grade, our science teacher would bring my classmates and I to the Audio Visual (AV) room to watch a science documentary from America on a CRT TV, the largest, I think, that we could get in the Philippines during the 90s. 

Meanwhile, the films were in VHS and looked and sounded old for us children, but we enjoyed going there, because the room was air-conditioned, unlike our classrooms, which only used electric fans for ventilation.

One time, our science teacher was explaining to us that the sun could only really last up to a certain point in time before it would truly disappear, and everything would be dark and cold, and all the living things on planet Earth would die. 

But, he quickly pointed out, it would still be a very long time, many millions of years, before this event would take place. By that time, my classmates agreed, we'd be dead. After that lecture, except maybe once, I never saw our science teacher again. 

As it turned out, he was one of the many grade school teachers that I had who went missing after making a pronouncement like that. One, a female teacher, who taught Christian Life Education (CLE) and then English, spoke about Satan actually appearing before us and whom we'd have to confront; our life would depend on our decision, she said.

After this incident, another CLE teacher spoke confidently that once she had died and could come back from the dead, she'd meet us and tell this to us. It was during a lecture in which she was explaining the difficulty for ants to understand human beings, as it was for human beings to understand God, hence the need for Jesus Christ.

Now, as to my answer why there are people who'd walk toward a wall and just stand there in front of it for hours on end, this was actually part of my spiel to my younger sibling, who I thought had gotten himself stuck, kind of like someone with a locked jaw, and needed advice. I thought that he needed to be aware that he's very much overweight, a common sight among fathers. 

Since he didn't really know why people did things like stand in front of walls, I told him that there are people who do things, because they think that they need to do them. The person who stands in front of a wall thinks that he needs to do it. 

In the end, I told him that I had to say something, instead of remaining silent despite already seeing problems coming  within a decade or so, because after all he's read and watched online, he's chosen to eat food that makes him very overweight. 

This is all. 

Thanks very much.

Regards,

Mike
