> Publication Date: 2026-10-14

Dear friends,

Today let me share with you Mr. Yoshiki Okamoto's lecture four days ago about the new expansion Monster Hunter Wilds: Ascendance.

## --

First, Okamoto-san announces that the Monster Hunter Wilds: Ascendance game has received Japan's Game of the Future Award for the year 2026. In addition, he points out that the word "Ascendance" has many meanings depending on the language, such as "an evolution" or "a rise" in English, while it can mean "ancestry" or "origin" in French.

Okamoto-san next recounts that the base game Monster Hunter Wilds has sold 10 million copies, although after this rise, the sales have gone down dramatically. He thinks that it's because it lacks something important, also emphasizing that he's changed his two PS5 consoles for two of the Pro version, in addition to buying two 4K monitors, so that he could put them side-by-side, specifically to play Monster Hunter Wilds.

However, after finishing it in 10 hours, he's not again returned to play it. Despite this, Okamoto-san is again very excited to turn on his two PS5 Pros to try out the new expansion.

Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that he's continued to play the franchise's mobile version called "Monster Hunter NOW," although not as much as before. Also, because he really likes this series, he'd like to give encouragement to Capcom, naming Mr. Ryozo Tsujimoto as a key figure of the franchise, also highlighting that the development team will have to surpass 10 million unit sales by achieving 15 million to 20 million.

Before closing, Okamoto-san says that he also wants to read our comments about this topic in case we have something to say.

I'd thus like to respond that increasing the number of unit sales by buying the studio's own game is a technique that has also spread to foreign studios. However, many studios still continue to go bankrupt based on the news reports from video game websites. Therefore, I think that when video game creators or similar have spent their money without recuperating it as expected, they'll resort to the family real estate, something very common, and which I've seen many times.
