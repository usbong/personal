According to Okamoto-san, Bangkok has this problem for various reasons. For example, the country's inclined topography makes typhoon water move faster downward, so much so that the embankment couldn't contain it in this occasion, causing the flood to form much faster as well. In fact, the height of the water reached people's waists, and a resident would need a full month to clean his house.

In addition, the current state of the drainage system contributed to the problem, especially due to the cigarettes that the people continued to throw into the drainage, because they thought that these won't catch fire. However, as a result, a blockage was created, and because of this, the drainage system couldn't prevent more water from accumulating. Also, these cigarettes became stickier, such that the trash also piled up more easily.

Okamoto-san therefore believes that the embankment has to be made higher, the drainage system strengthened by not allowing trash including, in particular, cigarettes to be thrown in it, and the city cleaned.
