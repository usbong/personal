First, Okamoto-san shares with us a news report in France about warehouse refrigerators that had been hacked by a malicious group that used AI to change the temperature to 50 degrees celcius. Okamoto-san says that this type of frightening situation is now what we can encounter today due to AI.

Okamoto-san next recounts that when he was in poverty 12 years ago, he ate once or twice a week at a restaurant near the Hiyoshi train station in the Kanto Prefecture, because they offered two "teishoku" set meals for the price of one. He also recalls that Keio University's rugby team also went there, and given that it was a two-floor building, customers by themselves like salarymen (regular employees at a company) ate at the first floor, while those who numbered more than one person ate at the second floor. While the restaurant seemed to have already disappeared, he very much liked to go there not because the food was particularly delicious, but because the price was rather cheap, especially in order to obtain calories.

Okamoto-san also speaks about a tempura restaurant near Capcom to which he frequented, so much so that the owner already knew him and would immediately prepare meals for Okamoto-san's entire team. Unfortunately, this restaurant disappeared after Okamoto-san retired from Capcom.

Okamoto-san then asks Ms. Tanu as well as Ms. Tapi about the restaurants that they'd recommend.

For example, Ms. Tanu gives well-known restaurants in Japan such as Saizeriya, Bikkuri Donkey (for their hamburger patty and ice cream), and Mister Donut.

Ms. Tapi, for her part, gives lesser known restaurants found in her hometown of Hakata in Osaka City such as Yucanti (an Italian restaurant), Hakata Ramen (owned by her classmate and whose ramen can be found in the Ramen Museum in Yokahama City), and another that mixes hotpot with steamed buns.

Finally, Okamoto-san mentions that he had a junior colleague who worked at Mister Donut part-time while he went to Game Republic, the teacher's video game studio. It was a time when Okamoto-san and team were experiencing difficulties with respect to money, and that colleague of his would bring surplus Mister Donut donuts that he could get hold of after closing the store, because there would always be 300 extra donuts prepared and which would cost the store 10 yen each.

This is all. Of course, there's always much more to say, but I should already stop here.
