This time around, Kenny-san tells us that after training as an agriculturist in an apple farm in Aomori, Tohoku Prefecture, he moved to Tokyo to pursue his original goal of managing a food and drinks store. He also reveals that Kimura-san, who was his teacher in Aomori, introduced him to businessmen who wanted someone who could manage the shops that they had and in which they had invested.

Kenny-san then reveals that he was 30 or 31 years old when he began to work as a manager who looked into both the original prices of their products and their sales prices when they sold them to customers. During that time, he says that he understood more the whole business and how its several components fit together.

Thus, when he was 36 or 37 years old, a female acquaintance had invited him to be a partner in the entertainment industry, so that he again returned to the world of entertainers which he had previously left behind. However, this time, he was able to understand more the business just like in the food and drinks industry, in that it had several components such as merchandises and fanbases, in addition to young women. Further still, he recalls an instance when he joined for the first time a female group's event, and he realized that it was really a wonderful industry in which many people would come and would buy the group's merchandises. It was a lot better than he had previously thought.

Finally, Kenny-san mentions that in those years when an entertainer created a YouTube channel, it became big news for the industry, so much so that he was given a position at Cave to lead this very task.
