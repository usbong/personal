Kenny-san recounts that after graduating from high school, when he was 20 years old, he was able to find an operations job ("dandori") at a warehouse. He explains that he had wanted to create his own business since some time ago, though he knew that he needed to save money first. He was also able to get a driver's license relatively faster through another, less usual place thanks to his work, something that Okamoto-san doesn't advise us immitating.

Next, when he was 21 years old, he entered a host club bar, because it seemed to him that it was the fastest way to earn money, given that he was thinking about creating his own business at 25 years old. However, although he was working in a host club, he reveals that he didn't want to establish this type of business at all.

Then, when he was 23 years old, he moved to the real estate industry in Tokyo, in which he called up clients on the telephone. With regard to this point, Okamoto-san shares that he had experienced receiving telephone calls about a dormant account that he had, and which the caller was asking him if he wanted to re-activate it. He recalls having told the female caller that he'd deposit 100 million yen, which led to him talking to the manager based in Kyushu and who became very respectful toward Okamoto-san. He explains that it was certainly necessary to first verify the person's identity.

When Kenny-san reached 25 years old, he already had quite a sum of money, amounting to as much as 20 million yen, to establish a business. This time around, he returned to the bar industry, in particular, host clubs. He further admits that he met Yakuza gangsters who came to the host club. And in order for them not to come, Kenny-san spoked with them in a nearby coffee shop, while apologizing that his business was an honest one and which wasn't related to anything illegal. Kenny-san reveals that after persisting with this explanation, the gansters would go somewhere else.

With respect to this point, Okamoto-san points out that he himself has met gangsters when he was managing a hostess club, and it was really very difficult to communicate with them, especially because the person couldn't advise them anything, and if he tried to, they'd want to test his will.

Afterward, Kenny-san says that when he was 28 years old, he again moved to another industry, which was, in this case, in the food and drinks industry. Thus, he went to train as an agriculturist, another job, in which, just like before when he was still 20 years old, he would be able to learn many things. 

This is all. I end here this part of the conversation between Okamoto-san and Kenny-san.
