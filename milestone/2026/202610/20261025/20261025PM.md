First, Okamoto-san announces that he's very excited to hear this news, given that he respects Miyamoto-san very much. He also says that the Emperor himself will be officially awarding Miyamoto-san this November 3 at the Imperial Palace.

Okamoto-san next reveals that he discovered for the first time the potential of video games when Miyamoto-san launched the game, Donkey Kong. During that time, he points out that there was no video game "industry" yet. 

Finally, after many years and many words used to identify the roles of people who work in this industry, Okamoto-san concludes that everyone in the video game industry including the magazine writers, gamers as well as video game creators are surely satisfied that Miyamoto-san has won this medal and not someone else.

This is all. Okamoto-san also advises us to write our positive comments about this news.

I'd therefore like to say that I'm very happy to see Miyamoto-san again, and I admit that while I played a lot of CAPCOM's Street Fighter Zero 3, I'd choose to work at Nintendo in the 2000s for the good memory that I had about its family-friendly games.
