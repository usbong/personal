## --

Since August of this year, the forest fire in Indonesia has continued to worsen, affecting even the orangutans living in the forest and who are in danger of extinction. While the government has been trying to put out the fire, as of late October, they haven't yet gotten the fire under control.

Forest fire in Indonesia worsens, injuring even wild orangutans

## --

The rescuers tried to rescue the orangutan "Nobita" by putting out the fire nearby and letting her fall from the tree, but because of poor visibility due to the smoke, they abandoned their rescue operation on that day. The next day, Nobita was put to sleep using a tranquilizer gun. She fell from the tree and was caught by the net that they had prepared beforehand. 

Nobita's foot and hands were burned, and her breathing was impaired after having inhaled the smoke. She was also seen to have severe dehydration, and was given an emergency oxygen mask and an intravenous drip, among other things, by the veterinarian on site. Afterward, she was transferred to the foundation's rehabilitation facility.
