Ms. Tanu shares that a new hotel has been produced by the collaboration between the Pokémon franchise and NOT A HOTEL. The hotel has the shape of a Pokéball with a diameter of only three meters. Ms. Tanu also informs us that the hotel is in Hokkaido and is only open to specific people, including those who've won the lottery, which is to end today. In other words, it's currently not yet available to everyone.

Also, the participants of the lottery must have the mobile app Pokémon Sleep, a game in which Pokémon creatures sleep each time the user does. Sr. Okamoto then admits that the game progresses very slowly in his case, because he sleeps very little. Because of this, he thinks that it's really a game for children. On the other hand, he says taht the mobile game Pikmin Bloom may be more suitable for him, because it's an app about walking, something that he does every morning as his exercise. However, he reveals that he had stopped the use of this app, because he's in a province where no event related to the game happens, though he thinks about playing it again.

Tanu-san next says that the lottery winners don't need to pay anything aside from commuting to the place. Furthermore, she says that while the bathroom as well as the restaurant are outside the hotel, they can be reached by foot in only five minutes in the case of the restaurant. There's also a lake in front of the hotel, and given that the hotel is transparent, the person could enjoy the scenery.

Finally, Tanu-san reveals that if an applicant couldn't win the lottery, he'll get a serial code that he can use in the app to obtain a special item. This means that all the participants can get something by just participating.
