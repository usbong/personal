According to data, there are 130 million up to 140 million players of Roblox. It's a number so huge that Okamoto-san wants to strive to launch a bestselling title.

In addition, Okamoto-san reveals that while grownups don't know much about this game, this isn't so for the children who not only play Roblox games but also create them. This is why there are tens of millions of games on Roblox including indie games or games created by non-professional developers. 

With respect to this point, Okamoto-san reminds Ms. Tanu that they've tried creating some Roblox games before, although Okamoto-san says that he still hasn't used any marketing and such for these games, developing what he himself would want. This, he says, is really part of the steps that he wants to take to launch a successful title on Roblox.

Okamoto-san also announces that there'll be an event in which Roblox fans will be able to try out and play the most recognized games on Roblox throughout its 20 years of existence.

Furthermore, Okamoto-san emphasizes that, as what the data say, Roblox is what players would choose to play, much more than Monster Hunter Wilds or the remake of Zelda for Switch 2.

Finally, Okamoto-san concludes that he wants to continue playing Roblox until he succeeds. He also wants to read our comments about this topic.

I'd thus like to say that I view Okamoto-san's videos in order to understand more the video game business and how the producers of these games could make money. I think that given that Okamoto-san has worked in this industry for decades, he, of course, knows ways, including those related to marketing, to be able to survive longer than other people. However, I think that Okamoto-san himself also wants to understand how to launch a successful game that will be able to give him money in the present time. Meanwhile, we may recall that video game creators can use the Unity Engine to create Roblox games, and that Okamoto-san still hasn't announced to us that he's already sold his Unity shares costing millions of yen.
