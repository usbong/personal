This time around, Kenny-san tells us that when he was 28 years old, he left the host club industry in order to train as an agriculturist. He says that he went to Aomori in the Tohoku (Northeast) region to learn to cultivate apples for two years. He explains that he wanted to do another job after running a host club in Kabuki-cho, Osaka City.

Moreover, he points out that he was receiving 200,000 yen each month as salary, and admits that during that time, money wasn't as important as the experience of being with good colleagues, who could care for each other through work, eating and going out together to drink sake in the local pub. He also recalls that the teacher, Mr. Kimura, told them what he was doing in the past like stories with jokes that were very amusing.

With regard to the work itself, they were farmers who had to cultivate apples by hand. For example, an apple could have a good color red in the middle, but not so much above or below, so they had to move the leaves, so that sunlight could reach the parts that were less red. They started working from six in the morning until five in the afternoon, and Kenny-san would sometimes go out with his colleagues in the evening whenever they would invite him.

Finally, Kenny-san concludes that learning to cultivate apples continues and doesn't stop, and since he had originally wanted to run a food and beverages store, he transferred to a company in Tokyo after two years.
