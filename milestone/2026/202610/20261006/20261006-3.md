> South Korean Police investigate cyberattacks against banks

> Translator: [Michael B. Syson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelsyson/)

> Publication Date: 2026-10-06

## --

Authorities consider cyberattacks to be proof that current security measures are no longer effective.

## --

The South Korean Police decided to open an investigation about a series of cyberattacks against several financial institutions, including the country's main banks, which suffered customer information leakages, according to the announcement made by the authorities of the Asian country this Tuesday (Oct. 6, 2026).

Seoul formed a team of 28 members to investigate the case, which affects Hana Bank, KB Kookmin and Shinhan, among others, as it studies whether or not to dispatch them to the investigating agency for serious crimes, according to South Korean news agency, Yonhap. 

Prime Minister Han Seong-sook requested for a holistic reform in the nation's security system saying, "This is a serious situation, because it's believed that this incident was made using Artificial Intelligence (AI), and if this is used for 'phising' attacks, it could cause collateral damage."
