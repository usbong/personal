Okamoto-san begins by revealing that Kenny-san has attained this very high position of CEO from a very low beginning, that is, from poverty. Kenny-san explains that before moving to Osaka City, he lived in a part of Kawasaki City in Kanagawa Prefecture, which, we may, was still a province, because their very small house, he says, had been at the center of a pear plantation.

Also, while his father worked in a company that did operations, they didn't have a lot money, so they survived through food like the "kinmai" or the "sakurai rice," which they would get from their friends. These rice were very delicious for Kenny-san.

Kenny-san continues that when he was in 5th or 6th grade, there was a conversation about buying a new house. Shortly thereafter, the family's house suddenly caught fire and was very quickly burned to the ground.
