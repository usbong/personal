Okamoto-san begins by saying that Minecraft has been downloaded 452 million times in its entire life, since 17 years ago. Furthermore, the game's sole developer has earned a lot of money if each copy was sold during those years for 3,000 yen. Moreover, he's gotten even more money after his studio was acquired by Microsoft.

Okamoto-san also highlights that this number that Minecraft has achieved is very significant, because the first entry of the Resident Evil series, which Capcom released in order to survive in the 90s, only needed to sell 300,000 copies, although it was eventually able to sell 5.5 million copies through the years.

Okamoto-san also points out that Minecraft continues to be played by thousands of people every day, a very incredible figure, and has received several updates including the addition of stages such as outer space and hell in addition to Earth.

Okamoto-san next compares it with another best-selling game, GTA V, which has sold 200 million copies. This then means that if an indie game like Minecraft can sold so much, comparable or much better than other games from big studios, we live in a very favorable time to release indie games.

Finally, I'd like to share Okamoto-san's answer to his viewers about the use of AI by countries against other countries, in which he emphasizes that the technology is used to attack computer systems that launch rockets, among other things; therefore, the capability to command AI is very essential today.
